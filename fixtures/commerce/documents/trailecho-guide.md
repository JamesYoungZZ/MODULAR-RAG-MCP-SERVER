## Indoor training classification
TrailEcho is listed as an indoor training shoe. It has a textile upper and rubber sole, with red and black catalog colors. Trail is part of the model name; the current product record does not place this shoe in the trail-running category.
## Before entering the studio
Remove outdoor dirt from the sole before using shared exercise floors. Secure the laces so they cannot catch on equipment. Follow the studio's requirements for footwear; the product record does not supply a non-marking or specialist court-use certification.
## Cleaning after exercise
Use a soft brush for loose dust and a damp cloth with mild soap for the textile upper. Remove soap residue and let the pair dry in a ventilated room. Do not machine wash, submerge the shoe or dry it on a heater.
## Fit and training limits
Try lateral steps indoors to assess whether your foot slips within the shoe. The guide does not assign a safe lifting weight or a sport-specific stability rating. Stop an activity if the shoe is damaged or no longer fits securely.
