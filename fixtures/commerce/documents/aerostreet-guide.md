## Running construction
AeroStreet is a running-category model with a synthetic upper and rubber sole. Black is its catalog color. Street is part of the model name; the product record describes a road-running style rather than a separate lifestyle collection.
## Wiping the upper
Remove loose grit first, then wipe the upper with a soft damp cloth. Use mild soap sparingly and remove residue from seams. Do not use solvent cleaners, abrasive pads or machine washing. Avoid immersing the complete shoe.
## Drying after rain
Open the lacing area and allow the lining to dry at room temperature. Keep the pair away from hot-air dryers and radiators. The synthetic upper is not, by itself, proof of waterproofing; this guide includes no waterproof test result.
## Fit and maintenance limits
Try both shoes with running socks before outdoor use. Check toe room without overtightening the laces. The publication does not specify a replacement mileage, width measurement or material-based guarantee of breathability.
