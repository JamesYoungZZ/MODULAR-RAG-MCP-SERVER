## Everyday use
MotionGrid is a lifestyle model with an engineered knit upper and foam midsole. The white and sand versions use the same instructions. It is intended for everyday casual wear; this publication does not specify running or gym performance standards.
## Protecting the knit
Release the laces before taking the shoe off. Avoid pulling on a single knit loop or catching the upper on hooks and rough edges. If a loop snags, do not cut it or pull it tighter; have the damaged area assessed before further wear.
## Spot cleaning
Press a soft damp cloth against dirty areas and use mild soap if needed. Wipe away residue without soaking the assembled shoe. Do not machine wash or wring out the upper. Dry the shoe at room temperature with the opening unobstructed.
## Shape and fit
Store the pair without heavy objects resting on it. The knit construction is not a promise that the shoe will stretch enough to accommodate any foot width. Choose fit through an indoor try-on; no width measurements are available in this guide.
