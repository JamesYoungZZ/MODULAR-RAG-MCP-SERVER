## Construction and use

CloudStep Runner has a recycled mesh upper and rubber outsole. It is a road-running style for everyday use on maintained surfaces. The material description is not a waterproof rating or a guarantee of comfort for every wearer.

## Cleaning and drying

Do not machine wash or tumble dry this shoe. Remove loose dirt with a soft brush, then spot clean the upper with a damp cloth and mild soap. Do not soak the assembled shoe. Air dry at room temperature away from radiators and direct heat.

## Insole and fit

The insoles are removable. Remove them before cleaning the inside and allow them to dry separately. Compatibility with any particular orthotic has not been assessed. Try both shoes with the socks intended for use; a removable insole alone does not establish suitability for a foot condition.

## Size selection and limits

Sizes in the product catalogue use the EU system. A size label does not establish foot width or fit. No model-specific internal-length or width measurement is published in this guide. Current available sizes, colors and stock must be checked in the selling market; this document contains no availability promise.
