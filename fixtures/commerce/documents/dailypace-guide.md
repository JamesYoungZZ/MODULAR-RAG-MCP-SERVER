## Everyday walking
DailyPace belongs to the walking collection and has an engineered knit upper and foam midsole. Its catalog colors are navy and white. The guide describes everyday walking use without promising a specific number of comfortable hours for each wearer.
## Protecting the upper
Loosen the laces before putting on or removing the shoe. Keep the knit away from hook fasteners and rough edges that may catch loops. Do not cut a snagged strand; assess the damage rather than pulling it through the surrounding fabric.
## Care between wears
Spot clean with a soft damp cloth and mild soap. Remove residue by blotting with clean water on the cloth, then air dry. Do not machine wash, wring or tumble dry. Store the pair only after the inside has dried.
## Fitting guidance
Try both shoes with everyday socks and check the heel and forefoot during an indoor walk. Knit material does not establish a wide-fit designation. There is no published internal-width chart or guaranteed break-in period for this model.
