# EchoStride | Product and care guide

## Product construction
EchoStride has a textile upper and rubber sole and is listed for running. Black and white are the catalog color choices. The same use and care instructions apply to both colors; exact color-size stock comes from the inventory service.
## Cleaning textile and laces
Brush off dry dirt, then wipe the upper gently using a damp cloth and mild soap. Laces can be removed for separate hand washing. Refit them only when dry. Do not machine wash the assembled shoe or use bleach on the textile.
## Drying after use
Loosen the laces and open the tongue while the pair dries naturally. Keep the shoes away from radiators and tumble dryers. If moisture remains inside, leave them out of their storage box until the lining has dried.
## Size selection
Use an indoor try-on to check both length and heel movement with the intended socks. The guide does not provide a measured width or a model-specific EU-to-US conversion. A familiar number in another size system must not be treated as a verified conversion.
