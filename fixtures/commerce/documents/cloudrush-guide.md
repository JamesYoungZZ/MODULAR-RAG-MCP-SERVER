# CloudRush | Product and care guide

## Indoor training scope
CloudRush is an indoor training shoe with an engineered knit upper and foam midsole. Its red and black catalog options share this care guide. The published construction does not specify a weightlifting load limit or a running-distance recommendation.
## Preparing for a session
Check that laces are secured and that no loose grit is trapped under the shoe before stepping onto a studio floor. Follow the venue's footwear rules. A clean sole is not evidence of non-marking certification, which has not been supplied for this model.
## Knit care
Clean small marks by pressing gently with a damp cloth rather than brushing vigorously across the knit loops. Use mild soap sparingly, wipe away residue and air dry. Do not machine wash, twist the upper to remove water or accelerate drying with a hairdryer.
## Condition and replacement
Inspect the foam and the joins around the shoe after use. If the sole separates or the upper tears, stop using the damaged shoe for training and seek an assessment. This guide sets no mileage or month-based replacement interval; wear depends on use and storage.
