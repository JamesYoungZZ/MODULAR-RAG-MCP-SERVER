## Upper cleaning

Hand clean surface marks with a soft cloth and a small amount of mild soap. Test the cleaning solution on an inconspicuous area first. Do not immerse the assembled shoe or put it in a washing machine. Avoid stiff brushes on the textile upper.

## Drying and storage

After cleaning, reshape the shoe lightly and air dry in a ventilated place. Do not place it on a heater or use a tumble dryer. Store only after it is dry; prolonged storage in a damp gym bag can retain moisture.
