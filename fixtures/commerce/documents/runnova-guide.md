## Trail use
RunNova is a trail-category model with a synthetic upper and rubber sole. It is offered in black and green catalog colors. Use this guide for general care after recreational unpaved-path use; it does not describe technical climbing equipment.
## Cleaning the outsole
Remove loose stones from the tread with a soft brush or a blunt cleaning tool, taking care not to cut the rubber. Wipe the sole separately from the upper. Leave dried mud to loosen before cleaning rather than forcing a sharp tool into grooves.
## Upper care
Wipe the synthetic upper with a damp cloth and mild soap. Avoid solvents, abrasive pads, machine washing and prolonged immersion. Let the shoe air dry with the tongue open. Do not use a radiator to dry a mud-soaked pair quickly.
## Conditions to check
Wet rock, ice and loose ground require their own assessment. The trail category does not provide a slip-proof guarantee, and no waterproof rating is published. Check fit with the intended socks and inspect the sole for damage before an outing.
