## Running construction
MotionPeak is a running-category shoe with a recycled mesh upper and rubber sole. Grey and blue are catalog colors. Peak is part of the model name; it is not a declaration of technical mountain or climbing suitability.
## Mesh and sole cleaning
Brush dry dirt off the sole first. Clean the mesh upper with a soft damp cloth and mild soap, then remove residue. Avoid stiff brushes, machine washing and prolonged soaking. Do not scrape seams with a sharp tool.
## Drying after use
Loosen the laces and leave the pair to dry naturally in a ventilated space. Keep it away from direct heat and do not seal it in a damp bag. No waterproof treatment or water-resistance test is published for this model.
## Fit and information limits
Try both shoes with running socks indoors before outdoor use. Check toe room and heel movement rather than choosing by the model name. The guide has no published internal-width measurement, orthotic-compatibility assessment or fixed replacement mileage.
