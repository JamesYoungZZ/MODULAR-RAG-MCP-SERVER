## Product overview
AirStride is a running-category model with a textile upper and rubber sole. Grey and blue are its catalog colors. Its name does not identify an air-cushion component; no such component is listed in the published construction.
## Cleaning the upper
Clean surface dust with a soft brush while the shoe is dry. For remaining marks, use a damp cloth with mild soap and wipe again with a clean cloth. Avoid prolonged soaking and machine washing. Do not scrape the textile with a stiff brush.
## Odor and storage
Allow the inside to dry fully between wears and store the pair in a ventilated place. Never seal damp shoes in a plastic bag. No odor-control or antibacterial treatment is specified; fragranced sprays should not be described as a repair for damp storage.
## First wear
Adjust the laces while standing and check that the tongue lies flat. Try a short indoor walk before outdoor use to identify rubbing or slipping. There is no published break-in period after which an uncomfortable fit is guaranteed to improve.
