# CoreMotion | Product and care guide

Document: coremotion-guide | Version: 1 | Effective: 2026-10-09

Fictional product information authored for integration testing.

## Intended activity
CoreMotion is a road-running style with a synthetic upper and rubber sole. Grey and blue are catalog colors. The product record does not describe a stability device or a motion-control medical feature; those should not be inferred from the name.
## Cleaning the panels
Use a damp cloth to lift ordinary dirt from the synthetic upper. Apply mild soap to the cloth for marks and wipe again to remove it. Avoid solvents and abrasive cleaning pads. Do not machine wash or submerge the shoe.
## Drying and checks
Let the pair dry naturally with the tongue open. Before wearing it again, check the sole bond and remove grit from underneath. Direct heat can change materials and bonds, so do not dry on a radiator or with hot air.
## Lace adjustment and fit
Adjust laces evenly while both shoes are on. Excessive tension is not a solution for an unsuitable width or length. This publication does not provide internal dimensions or establish compatibility with a particular orthotic or foot condition.
