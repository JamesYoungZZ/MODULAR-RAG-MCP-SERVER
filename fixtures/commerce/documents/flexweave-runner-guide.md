## Walking use
FlexWeave Runner belongs to the walking collection. Its textile upper and rubber sole are intended for everyday walking, errands and commuting. Runner is part of the name and does not change that published use. The catalog offers navy and white color options; availability is a separate live check.
## Textile cleaning
Brush loose dust from the dry upper, then wipe one small area at a time with a damp cloth. Use mild soap for marks and remove the soap with a second clean cloth. Avoid bleach, solvent stain removers and machine washing. Scrubbing one patch aggressively may change its appearance.
## Laces and drying
Untie the shoe fully before removing it so the heel is not repeatedly crushed. Laces may be hand washed separately and dried before rethreading. Air dry the assembled shoe away from heaters; do not tumble dry or put it away in a closed bag while damp.
## Trying the size
Walk indoors in both shoes with your normal socks before deciding on fit. Check heel movement and pressure across the forefoot. This guide has no model-specific width measurement or foot-length conversion table; it cannot select an exact size from foot length alone.
