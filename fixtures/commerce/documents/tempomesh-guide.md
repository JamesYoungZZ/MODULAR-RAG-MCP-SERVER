## Construction
TempoMesh is a running model with a textile upper and rubber sole. Black is the catalog color. Although Mesh appears in its name, the maintained material description is textile; no pore-size, fabric-weight or ventilation test result is published.
## Cleaning black textile
Remove dry dust with a soft brush, then wipe with a clean damp cloth. Use mild soap only where needed and remove the residue. Do not apply bleach or polish to recolor faded textile. Machine washing is not part of the published care procedure.
## Drying routine
Open the tongue and laces and dry indoors at room temperature. Do not put the shoe on a heated drying surface or leave it in a closed bag while damp. Allow the lining to dry as well as the visible upper.
## Running fit
Try both shoes with your running socks and adjust lace tension evenly. Check for toe contact and heel slip during an indoor walk. No automatic size-up rule, width variant or prescribed break-in distance is supplied for this model.
