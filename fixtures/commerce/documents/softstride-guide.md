# SoftStride | Product and care guide

## Published use
SoftStride is a running-category shoe with a textile upper and rubber sole. Grey and blue are catalog colors. Soft is part of the model name; no softness measurement or guarantee of comfort for a particular wearer is published.
## Textile maintenance
Brush off dry particles before wiping marks with a damp cloth and mild soap. Avoid scrubbing one spot so hard that its texture changes. Remove residue carefully. Do not bleach or machine wash the assembled shoe.
## Drying and routine handling
Untie the shoe before removing it and keep the heel from being crushed. Air dry the pair at room temperature with the opening clear. Avoid tumble dryers and radiators. Put it into storage only after both the lining and upper are dry.
## Trying the fit
Check for rubbing, pressure and heel movement during an indoor walk with both shoes on. An uncomfortable fit is not guaranteed to improve after a fixed break-in period. No foot-width or internal-length measurements are supplied in this guide.
