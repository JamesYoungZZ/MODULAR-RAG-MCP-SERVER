## Casual footwear scope
PulseRide is a lifestyle shoe with a textile upper and rubber sole. White and sand are the catalog color options. Its intended use is everyday casual wear; the model name is not a declaration of running suspension or a measured cushioning system.
## Cleaning pale textile
Remove dry dirt first, then wipe the upper in small areas with a clean damp cloth. Use mild soap for stubborn marks and remove residue carefully. Avoid bleach, colored polishing products and harsh rubbing that can leave an uneven patch.
## Drying and storage
Do not machine wash or tumble dry the shoe. Let the pair dry naturally away from sunlight intense enough to heat the material. Store it after the interior is dry, with nothing compressing the toe or heel areas.
## Matching the occasion
Check the dress and safety requirements of the place where you will wear the shoe. This guide does not certify workplace protection or slip resistance. For size selection, try both shoes indoors; the textile material alone does not establish width or stretch.
