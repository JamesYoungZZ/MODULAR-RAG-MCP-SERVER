## Indoor exercise use
UrbanPulse is an indoor training model with an engineered knit upper and foam midsole. Red and black are catalog colors. Pulse is the model name; the product has no published heart-rate sensor or connected-device feature.
## Preparing the pair
Inspect the upper, sole join and laces before exercise. Remove outdoor grit from the underside before stepping onto studio mats. Follow the venue's rules because no non-marking certification or specialist court-use rating has been provided.
## Knit cleaning
Use a soft damp cloth and mild soap on small dirty areas. Blot rather than rubbing hard across loops. Do not machine wash, wring or tumble dry. Leave the tongue open while the shoe dries naturally in a ventilated space.
## Movement and fit
During an indoor try-on, assess heel security and whether the foot slips during a controlled sideways step. Foam construction does not establish a lifting-load rating or suitability for every exercise. There is no individualized injury-prevention claim in this guide.
