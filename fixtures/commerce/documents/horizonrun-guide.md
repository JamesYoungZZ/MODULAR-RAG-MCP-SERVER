## Walking use and materials
HorizonRun is a walking-category shoe with a synthetic upper and rubber sole. Navy and white are catalog colors. The published category supports everyday walking use; the word Run does not establish a running-event or distance specification.
## Upper maintenance
Remove surface dirt with a soft damp cloth. Use a small amount of mild soap for marks, then wipe it off. Do not use solvent cleaners, shoe bleach or a scouring pad. Avoid machine washing and soaking the assembled shoe.
## Drying before storage
If the shoe becomes wet, loosen the laces and dry it in a ventilated room. Keep it off radiators and out of tumble dryers. Store the pair with the heel and toe free from pressure that could deform the upper.
## Selecting a fit
Try both feet indoors and check that the heel stays seated without overtightening the laces. No foot-width measurement or EU-to-centimeter table is available in this publication. A prior size in another model is a starting point, not a fit guarantee.
