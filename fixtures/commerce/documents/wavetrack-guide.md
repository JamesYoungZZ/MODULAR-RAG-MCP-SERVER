## Panel cleaning
Wipe the upper using a soft cloth, water and mild soap. Clean visible dirt off before it dries into seams. Avoid solvent cleaners, abrasive pads and bleach. Do not machine wash the shoe or leave it submerged in cleaning water.
## Drying the pair
Let the shoe dry naturally with the laces loosened. Keep it away from direct heat, including heated drying racks. Make sure the interior has dried before storing the pair in a box; a dry outside does not establish that the lining is dry.
