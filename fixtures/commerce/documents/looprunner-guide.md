## Trail classification
LoopRunner is a trail model with a synthetic upper and rubber sole. Black and green are its catalog colors. Use it within the published recreational trail scope; no climbing, ice-traction or crampon-compatibility specification is supplied.
## Sole care after use
Remove stones and dried mud with a soft brush or blunt cleaning tool. Keep sharp objects away from the rubber and seams. Clean the underside before the upper to avoid transferring dirt back onto the shoe.
## Upper cleaning and drying
Wipe the synthetic panels with a damp cloth and mild soap. Avoid solvents, high-pressure water, machine washing and soaking. Open the tongue and dry the pair naturally in a ventilated room rather than on a radiator.
## Before another outing
Check for sole separation and worn areas before use. This guide does not specify a waterproof membrane or guaranteed grip on wet rock. Try fit with the intended socks and consider the actual terrain and weather when choosing footwear.
