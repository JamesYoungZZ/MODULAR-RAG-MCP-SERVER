## Published construction
LightForm is a road-running shoe with a recycled mesh upper and rubber sole. Its catalog colors are grey and blue. The product information does not publish a measured shoe weight, so the name cannot support a numeric weight comparison with another model.
## Gentle mesh care
Brush off dry dirt lightly and clean remaining marks with a damp cloth and mild soap. Do not use a stiff brush that can catch in the mesh. Wipe off soap residue. Machine washing and soaking the assembled shoe are not permitted by this care guide.
## Ventilation and drying
Leave the lacing area open while the shoe dries indoors. Keep the pair away from direct heat and do not place heavy objects on wet uppers. Ventilation helps drying; the material listing itself is not a certified breathability measurement.
## Fit check before use
Try the shoe indoors and check for pressure at the toes and heel movement. Use the same type of sock you plan to run in. There is no published guidance to automatically size up or down, and no individual fit guarantee.
