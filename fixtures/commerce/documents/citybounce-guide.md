## Walking and commuting
CityBounce is a walking model with a recycled mesh upper and rubber sole. Its navy and white color options are catalog choices, not a statement of stock. No quantitative rebound or energy-return measurement is published for the model despite its name.
## Dust and street marks
Brush off dry surface dust before using a damp cloth. Work mild soap gently into marks on the cloth, then remove residue with clean water on a second cloth. Do not machine wash, bleach or soak the complete shoe.
## Daily drying routine
After commuting in damp conditions, loosen the laces and allow the pair to dry indoors at room temperature. Keep it away from radiators and avoid stacking a wet pair inside another bag. Storage should start only after the lining feels dry.
## Comfort assessment
Try both shoes with the socks you expect to wear on a normal day. Walk indoors and check the heel, toe space and top of the foot. The walking category does not establish comfort for every wearer or for a particular medical condition.
