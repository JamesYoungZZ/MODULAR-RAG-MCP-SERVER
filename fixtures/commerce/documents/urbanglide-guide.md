## Knit care

Use a soft damp cloth and avoid scrubbing or snagging the knit. Do not use a stiff brush on the upper. Machine washing and tumble drying are not approved. Allow the assembled shoe to air dry without stretching the wet upper.

## Wet weather

No waterproof membrane or water-resistance rating is published. Knit appearance is not proof of ventilation performance, and a foam midsole is not proof of traction on wet floors. Use only the performance information specifically stated for this model.
