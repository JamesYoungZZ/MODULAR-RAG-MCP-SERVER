# Germany | Voluntary returns and promotion terms

Document: returns-de | Version: 2 | Effective: 2026-10-09

Fictional product and policy information for integration testing.

## Voluntary return window

For purchases in the German storefront, the fixture voluntary return window is 30 days from receipt. The product must remain unworn, with its original labels. This commercial policy summary does not replace applicable statutory rights or decide a defect claim.

## Indoor try-on versus outdoor wear

Careful indoor try-on to assess fit is consistent with the unworn-item condition when it leaves the item unworn and with its original labels. Outdoor walking that leaves wear or soiling does not satisfy this voluntary condition. An individual return decision requires inspection; the assistant must not promise approval.

## Promotional codes

Only one promotional code may be applied per order under this fixture policy. A publicly shown code does not establish current eligibility for every account or item. Current code status and any price calculation must come from the promotion or quote service, not this document.

## Escalation and privacy

For an order-specific defect, missing parcel or refund status, use the private support channel linked from the order account. Do not collect order numbers, addresses or payment details in public livestream comments. No refund timing or return shipping charge is specified in this document.
