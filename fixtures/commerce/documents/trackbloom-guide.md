# TrackBloom | Product and care guide

Document: trackbloom-guide | Version: 1 | Effective: 2026-10-09

## Casual-wear classification
TrackBloom is a lifestyle shoe with a synthetic upper and rubber sole. White and sand are catalog colors. Track is part of the name; no athletics-track or spike specification appears in the maintained product record.
## Caring for the finish
Wipe dust away with a soft cloth before cleaning marks using water and mild soap. Remove residue with another damp cloth. Avoid bleach, solvent cleaners and abrasive sponges, which are outside this published care method.
## Drying and shape retention
Do not machine wash or tumble dry the shoe. Air dry it away from direct heat and store it with the toe and heel free from pressure. Crushing a damp upper in luggage can change its shape; unpack and ventilate it first.
## Fit and workplace use
Try both shoes indoors before deciding on fit. This publication contains no slip-resistance or occupational-protection certification. A rubber sole alone is not enough to confirm that the shoe meets a workplace footwear requirement.
