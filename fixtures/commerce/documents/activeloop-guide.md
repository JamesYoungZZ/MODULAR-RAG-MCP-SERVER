## Trail-category construction
ActiveLoop is listed in the trail collection and uses an engineered knit upper with a foam midsole. Black and green are its catalog options. The current material record does not identify an outsole compound or publish tread-depth measurements.
## Preparing for outdoor use
Inspect the underside for wear and the upper for snags before each outing. Choose terrain appropriate to the conditions and your experience. This guide supplies no waterproof membrane, crampon-compatibility statement or technical mountain-use rating.
## Cleaning the knit
Let loose mud dry before lifting it gently from the upper. Use a damp cloth with mild soap for residue, without pulling at knit loops. Do not machine wash, pressure wash or twist the shoe. Air dry at room temperature.
## After the outing
Open the tongue and laces to help the inside dry. Do not store the pair in a closed wet bag. If a sole bond has separated, cleaning will not restore it; stop using the damaged pair and seek a product assessment.
