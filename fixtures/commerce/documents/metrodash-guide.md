## Road-running model
MetroDash has an engineered knit upper and foam midsole and belongs to the running collection. Black is its catalog color. The record does not publish a carbon plate, shoe weight or racing-distance specification.
## Putting on and removing
Loosen the laces enough to seat the heel without forcing the opening. Adjust the tongue and tighten evenly. Avoid using the other shoe to crush the heel when taking it off, and keep knit loops away from hooks and rough surfaces.
## Local cleaning
Blot dirt with a damp cloth and mild soap rather than scrubbing across the knit. Remove soap residue gently. Do not machine wash, wring out or tumble dry the shoe. Dry naturally with air reaching the inside.
## Checking fit and wear
Try both shoes with running socks indoors and check for heel slip and toe pressure. No measured width or guaranteed stretch allowance is published. Inspect the foam and sole join for damage instead of assuming a fixed mileage determines service life.
