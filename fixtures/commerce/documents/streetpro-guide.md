## Product classification

Despite the word Runner in its name, StreetPro Runner is classified as a lifestyle shoe in this catalogue. Its upper is synthetic and its sole is rubber. It is not documented here as a competition running shoe or protective workplace footwear.

## Care

Wipe the synthetic upper with a soft damp cloth and allow it to air dry. Avoid abrasive cleaners, bleach and solvent-based stain removers. Do not machine wash. Persistent stains should be assessed before applying a stronger cleaning product.

## Use limitations

No slip-resistance certification, electrical protection, waterproof membrane or safety-toe rating is declared. Do not infer suitability for a regulated workplace from the sole material. The guide also does not certify the complete shoe as vegan; a synthetic upper does not describe every adhesive or component.

## Color and size

Black and white are catalogue color options. This does not mean every color-size combination is currently offered or in stock in both markets. Use the exact variant record for a black EU 38 request. This guide gives no stock or price guarantee.
