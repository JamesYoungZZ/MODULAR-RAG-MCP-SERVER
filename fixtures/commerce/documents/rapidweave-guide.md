# RapidWeave | Product and care guide

Document: rapidweave-guide | Version: 1 | Effective: 2026-10-09

Fictional product information authored for integration testing.

## Training scope
RapidWeave is an indoor training shoe with a textile upper and rubber sole. Red and black are catalog colors. The name does not establish a speed benefit or a special woven-fiber performance rating beyond the material description.
## Before a session
Secure laces and check that the sole is clean before entering a shared workout space. Remove small stones picked up outdoors. The guide does not supply a non-marking certification or establish suitability for every studio floor.
## Textile cleaning
Use a soft brush for dry dust, then a damp cloth with mild soap for marks. Remove cleaning residue without soaking the shoe. Do not machine wash or tumble dry. Open the tongue and let the pair dry naturally after cleaning or exercise.
## Fit and condition
Try controlled movement indoors to check whether the heel lifts or the foot slides. Do not rely on tighter lacing to solve the wrong fit. A torn upper or separated sole requires assessment before further training; no fixed replacement interval is specified.
