## Training purpose
DashCraft is an indoor training model with a recycled mesh upper and rubber sole. Its catalog colors are red and black. The record does not specify a track-racing construction, despite a name that could suggest sprinting.
## Preparing the shoe
Check the laces and remove loose grit from the sole before a session. If coming in from outside, clean the underside before stepping onto shared mats. No certification for non-marking floors or a particular court sport is included in this guide.
## Mesh cleaning
Wipe the upper gently with a damp cloth and mild soap after removing surface dirt. Avoid brushing aggressively across the mesh. Do not machine wash or soak the assembled shoe. Dry with the opening clear so air can reach the interior.
## Training fit
During an indoor try-on, check heel security and forefoot pressure when standing and stepping sideways. This guide provides no prescribed lifting-load limit or individualized injury-prevention claim. Damaged seams or a separated sole need assessment before further use.
