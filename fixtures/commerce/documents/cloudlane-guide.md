## Product overview
CloudLane is a road-running model with a synthetic upper and rubber sole. Its catalog colors are black and white. No cloud-named cushioning technology is specified; the name alone cannot support a construction or performance claim.
## Panel and seam cleaning
Wipe loose dirt away with a soft damp cloth. Use mild soap for marks and remove residue from the seams. Do not use solvents, abrasive pads or machine washing. Avoid leaving the shoe immersed in cleaning water.
## Drying the interior
Open the tongue and laces after wet use and dry the pair at room temperature. Do not use a radiator or tumble dryer. Check that the lining has dried before closing the shoes inside a storage box.
## Size selection
Try both shoes indoors with the intended socks and check heel security as well as toe room. This guide supplies no model-specific foot-length conversion or advice to automatically buy a larger size. Live size and color availability must be checked separately.
