# GlideForm | Product and care guide

## Indoor training construction
GlideForm is an indoor training shoe with a synthetic upper and rubber sole. Red and black are catalog colors. Its name does not imply a sliding sole or suitability for activities that require specialist dance footwear.
## Preparing the sole
Check that the underside is clean and free of small stones before using an exercise floor. Secure laces and check the shoe for separation or tears. Follow venue footwear rules; a non-marking sole certification is not included in the product record.
## Cleaning the upper
Use a soft damp cloth and mild soap on marks. Remove cleaning residue rather than letting it dry into seams. Avoid solvents, abrasive pads, machine washing and soaking. Dry naturally with air reaching the interior.
## Fit during movement
During an indoor try-on, check whether the foot slips inside the shoe when taking a controlled sideways step. This guide does not assign a lifting-load rating or a sport-specific stability score. If fit is insecure, reassess the size instead of tightening indefinitely.
