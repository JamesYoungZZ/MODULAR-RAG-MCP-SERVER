## Running overview
NovaFlex is a road-running model with a recycled mesh upper and rubber sole. Black is its catalog color. The published data does not contain a measured flexibility score, so the name cannot be used for a numeric flexibility comparison.
## Mesh cleaning method
Remove dry debris with a soft brush before treating marks. Wipe with a damp cloth and mild soap and remove any residue. Do not machine wash, bleach or soak the complete shoe. Work gently around seams to avoid snagging the mesh.
## Drying and storage
Open the lacing area after wet use and dry at room temperature. Avoid heaters and hot-air dryers. Do not put the pair into a closed storage box until the lining is dry, and avoid storing heavy objects on the uppers.
## Fit and construction limits
