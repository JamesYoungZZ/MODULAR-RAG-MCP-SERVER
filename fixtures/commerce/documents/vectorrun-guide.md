## Running and materials
VectorRun uses a recycled mesh upper and rubber sole and is listed for running. Black and white are catalog colors. The material record does not state a whole-shoe recycled-content percentage or supply an environmental certification number.
## Removing surface dirt
Use a soft brush on dry dust and a damp cloth for remaining marks. Apply mild soap to the cloth and wipe away residue after cleaning. Do not machine wash or soak the shoe. Avoid bleach and stiff brushes that can damage the mesh.
## Drying between wears
Leave the laces loose and the tongue open in a ventilated room. Keep the pair off direct heat and out of a closed bag until the lining is dry. Do not use strong fragrance as a substitute for drying the interior.
## Fit and comparisons
Assess fit on both feet with the intended socks. This guide provides no heel-drop measurement, numeric cushioning score or brand-to-brand sizing equivalence. Comparisons on those properties require published measurements for both products.
