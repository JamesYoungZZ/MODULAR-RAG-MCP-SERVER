# France | Voluntary returns and promotion terms

Document: returns-fr | Version: 2 | Effective: 2026-10-09

Fictional product and policy information for integration testing.

## Voluntary return window

For purchases in the French storefront, the fixture voluntary return window is 30 days from receipt. Items must be unworn and retain their original labels. This commercial offer does not replace applicable statutory rights or establish the outcome of an individual defect claim.

## Condition and inspection

The original labels and unworn condition are required together; keeping the labels does not make a worn item eligible. Careful indoor try-on may be compatible with the policy if the item remains unworn. Outdoor wear or damage requires an individual assessment, and approval cannot be promised in a livestream reply.

## Discount combinations

The French storefront permits one promotional code per order in this fixture. A German storefront campaign is not automatically a French storefront campaign. Retrieve the current promotion for the selected market, and obtain an indicative quote if a discounted amount is requested.

## Private support

Order-specific return approval, delivery incidents and payment questions belong in authenticated private support. The public assistant may explain the published policy, but it cannot inspect an order or authorize a refund. No return-label fee or reimbursement deadline is established here.
