# FlexRoute | Product and care guide

Document: flexroute-guide | Version: 1 | Effective: 2026-10-09

## Casual-wear purpose
FlexRoute is a lifestyle model with a textile upper and rubber sole. White and sand are catalog colors. It is intended for everyday casual wear; Route does not establish a hiking, trail-running or long-distance walking certification.
## Cleaning the textile
Brush away loose dry dirt before using a damp cloth and mild soap on marks. Remove soap residue and avoid saturating seams. Do not bleach, machine wash or use abrasive cleaning pads. Cleaning does not repair fabric that has already torn.
## Drying and packing
Allow the pair to dry at room temperature with the opening clear. Do not tumble dry or place it on a heater. Before packing, make sure the lining is dry and keep heavy items from flattening the upper.
## Fit and choosing use
Try both shoes indoors and assess heel movement and forefoot pressure with your normal socks. No numeric flexibility measurement or extra-wide designation is published. For workplace or specialist activity requirements, this document supplies no safety certification.
