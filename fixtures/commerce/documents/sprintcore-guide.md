# SprintCore | Product and care guide

## Running construction
SprintCore is a road-running model with an engineered knit upper and foam midsole. Black is the listed catalog color. No plate construction, spike fittings or race-distance specification is included in the product information.
## Putting the shoe on
Open the laces sufficiently to avoid forcing the foot through a tight opening. Reposition the tongue and tighten the laces evenly. Pulling hard on the knit to enlarge the opening can distort it; the material is not a size-adjustment mechanism.
## Cleaning and drying
Use a soft damp cloth and a little mild soap for local marks. Blot the knit rather than scrubbing it. Do not machine wash, twist the upper or tumble dry the shoe. Leave it at room temperature with air reaching the interior.
## Checking wear
Examine the midsole and upper-to-sole join periodically. Visible separation or a torn upper needs assessment before continued running. This document does not provide a shoe weight, foam density, heel drop or fixed replacement mileage.
