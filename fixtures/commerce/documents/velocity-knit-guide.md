## Intended use and construction
Velocity Knit is a trail-category shoe with a recycled mesh upper and rubber sole. Use it for recreational outings on maintained unpaved paths. The word Knit is the model name; the material record specifies mesh. Inspect the path and choose a pace that allows secure footing.
## Removing mud
Let surface mud dry, then loosen it with a soft brush. Clean the mesh with a damp cloth and a small amount of mild soap. Work around the seam joining the upper and sole without pulling it. Do not machine wash, pressure wash or soak the shoe.
## Drying after an outing
Loosen the laces and open the tongue to allow air into the shoe. Dry at room temperature in a ventilated place. Replace damp packing paper rather than putting the shoe on a radiator. Brush stones out of the sole before the next use.
## Fit and surface limits
Try both shoes with the socks intended for walking and check for forward sliding on a gentle indoor incline. EU sizing does not specify foot width. No waterproof membrane, ice-traction rating or technical mountaineering use is specified for this model.
