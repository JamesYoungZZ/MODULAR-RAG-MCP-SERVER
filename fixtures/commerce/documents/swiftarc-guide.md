## Construction and intended use

SwiftArc Runner has a textile upper and rubber sole and is classified as a running style. The available specification does not identify a carbon plate or disclose a laboratory energy-return measurement.

## Routine care

Use a soft brush for dry dirt and a damp cloth for remaining marks. A mild soap solution may be used sparingly. Do not machine wash or dry with direct heat. Let the upper and lining dry fully before storage.

## Laces and replacement parts

Laces may be removed for separate hand cleaning. The shoe should not be soaked while the laces are cleaned. This guide does not list replacement-lace dimensions or offer a spare-parts ordering service; those details require a separate parts source.

## Fit and usage questions

There is no published wide-fit designation or guarantee of comfort over a full work shift. Ask for the wearer's intended use only when it would change the available guidance. Do not claim that textile construction alone makes the shoe appropriate for a medical or orthopedic need.
