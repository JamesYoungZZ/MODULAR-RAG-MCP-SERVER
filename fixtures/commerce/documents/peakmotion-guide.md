## Everyday casual model
PeakMotion is a lifestyle shoe with an engineered knit upper and foam midsole. White and sand are catalog colors. Its name does not identify alpine equipment; the current product description is for casual everyday use.
## Handling the knit upper
Open the laces before inserting the foot and avoid pulling the upper across rough edges. Knit loops can catch on hook fasteners. If one snags, do not cut it or pull it tight; inspect the affected area before continued wear.
## Cleaning and storage
Spot clean gently with a damp cloth and mild soap, then remove residue. Do not machine wash, wring or tumble dry the shoe. Let it dry at room temperature and store it without heavy objects flattening the upper.
## Use and fitting limits
This publication includes no waterproof rating, hiking-support specification or measured width. Try both shoes indoors for the intended casual use. Neither the foam midsole nor the knit upper guarantees that a particular foot shape will fit comfortably.
