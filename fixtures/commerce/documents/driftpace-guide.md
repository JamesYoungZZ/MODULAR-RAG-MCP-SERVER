## Walking construction
DriftPace is a walking shoe with a recycled mesh upper and rubber sole. Its catalog options are navy and white. The intended use is everyday walking; the product information does not prescribe a daily step count or therapeutic benefit.
## Mesh cleaning
Remove loose dirt with a soft brush and wipe localized marks with a damp cloth and mild soap. Work gently around the seams. Do not bleach, machine wash or soak the complete shoe. Remove soap residue before allowing it to dry.
## Keeping the pair dry
Loosen the laces after damp wear and leave the shoes in a ventilated room. Avoid radiators and hot-air dryers. Do not keep a damp pair inside a sealed bag; allow the lining to dry fully before storage.
## Fit and recycled-material wording
