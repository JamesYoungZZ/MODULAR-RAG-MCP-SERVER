# MotionLite | Product and care guide

## Road-running materials
MotionLite is a running shoe with an engineered knit upper and foam midsole. Grey and blue are catalog colors. No measured weight is published; Lite is a model name and does not establish that it is lighter than a named alternative.
## Care of knit loops
Handle the upper with a soft cloth rather than a stiff brush. If dirt has dried, loosen it gently before spot cleaning. Avoid pulling at caught loops. Do not cut a loose strand without an assessment of the damaged knit.
## Cleaning and drying
Use mild soap on a damp cloth, then blot away residue with a clean cloth. Do not machine wash, soak, wring or tumble dry the shoe. Leave it at room temperature with the laces loosened so the inside can dry.
## Before the next run
Check the upper-to-sole join and the condition of the foam before use. A damaged bond needs assessment; drying cannot repair it. The guide provides no prescribed replacement mileage or performance comparison with a different foam compound.
