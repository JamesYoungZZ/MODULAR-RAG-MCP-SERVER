## Product purpose
RoadMuse is a road-running style with a synthetic upper and rubber sole. Black is the catalog color for this model. Road use is the published category; the guide does not designate it as a track spike, court shoe or technical trail shoe.
## Surface cleaning
Use a soft damp cloth to remove street dust from the upper. Treat marks with diluted mild soap and wipe off residue before drying. Do not use acetone, alcohol-based cleaning solvents or an abrasive scouring pad on the synthetic panels.
## Wet-weather care
If the shoe becomes wet, open the tongue and let it dry naturally before storing it. Do not place it against a heater or tumble dry it. The listed synthetic material does not establish waterproofness; this guide contains no waterproof test result.
## Fit check
Fasten the laces evenly without using excessive tension to compensate for the wrong size. Try both shoes indoors with running socks and check that the toes are not pressed against the front. No width variant or internal-length chart is published here.
