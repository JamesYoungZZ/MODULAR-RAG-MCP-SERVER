## Construction and everyday running
PaceLine combines a recycled mesh upper with a rubber sole and is listed for road running. Black and white are catalog color options. Recycled describes the listed upper material; the record does not state a recycled percentage for the whole shoe.
## Caring for light-colored panels
Remove dry grit before wiping the mesh so it is not rubbed into the fabric. Apply a small amount of mild soapy water to a cloth, not directly into the shoe. Clean gently and remove residue. Do not use whitening bleach or machine wash the assembled shoe.
## After a wet run
Open the lacing area and leave the shoes in moving air at room temperature. Let the inside dry before the next wear. Avoid radiators, hot-air dryers and prolonged direct heat. Cleaning does not restore worn tread or repair a loose sole bond.
## Size and fit information
Use the EU size shown on the product listing as the size identifier, then assess fit on both feet. This publication has no guaranteed true-to-size or wide-fit claim. Live stock, including the chosen color and size, must be checked separately.
