## Everyday wear
RidgeStep is a lifestyle model with a recycled mesh upper and rubber sole. Its white and sand color choices share this guide. Ridge is a product name, not a declaration that the shoe is technical hiking equipment.
## Cleaning pale mesh
Allow dirt to dry before brushing it away gently. Work on marks with a damp cloth and mild soap, taking care not to snag the mesh. Do not bleach or machine wash the assembled shoe. Remove soap residue before drying.
## Travel and storage
Pack the shoes only after they are dry. Keep heavy items from flattening the uppers and separate dirty soles from clean clothing. If the pair becomes damp in a bag, unpack it promptly and dry it at room temperature.
## Choosing use and fit
This guide supplies no waterproof rating, ice-grip certification or ankle-support specification. Try both shoes indoors with the intended socks before outdoor wear. The EU size label identifies the size but does not quantify foot width.
