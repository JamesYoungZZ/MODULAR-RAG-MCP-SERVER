## Trail use and materials
CometRun is a trail-category shoe with a textile upper and rubber sole. Black and green are catalog colors. Its published use is recreational trail activity; no competition distance or speed-performance claim is supplied.
## Dirt removal
Brush away loose, dry mud from the sole before cleaning the upper. Wipe the textile with a damp cloth and mild soap. Avoid scraping seams with sharp objects, high-pressure water and machine washing. Cleaning the shoe does not restore worn tread.
## Drying after mud or rain
Open the tongue and lacing area and allow the pair to dry in a ventilated room. Do not use a heater or tumble dryer. Keep it out of a closed bag until the interior is dry, even when the outside looks clean.
## Terrain and fit
Check fit with the socks you intend to use outdoors. A trail label does not guarantee grip on ice or wet rock, and this model has no published waterproof result. Take the surface conditions into account when choosing footwear for an outing.
