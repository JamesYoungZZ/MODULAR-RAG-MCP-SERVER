# FlowMark | Product and care guide

## Walking collection
FlowMark is a walking shoe with a textile upper and rubber sole. Navy and white are catalog options. The published description supports everyday walking; it does not assign a daily distance target or a guaranteed standing-time benefit.
## Upper cleaning
Remove dry dirt using a soft brush, then clean localized marks with a damp cloth and mild soap. Work gently rather than saturating the textile. Remove soap residue. Do not machine wash, bleach or soak the assembled shoe.
## Lacing and drying
Untie the laces before removing the shoe so the heel is not repeatedly folded. If laces need cleaning, hand wash them separately. Let all parts dry at room temperature before storage; avoid tumble dryers and heated racks.
## Fit information
Check both feet in the shoes while standing and walking indoors. This guide contains no extra-wide designation or internal-length table. If the forefoot feels compressed, the textile material does not guarantee that wear will create enough room.
